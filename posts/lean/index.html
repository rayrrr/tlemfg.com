The Essence of Lean

on 2026-10-22

Lean manufacturing is often confused with agile project managment.

Lean manufacturing has nothing to do with daily standups, rolling planning, or sprints.

Put simply, the essence of lean manufacturing is about two things: continuous improvement and waste minimization.

Six Sigma gives us the tools for continuous improvement.

The Toyota Production System guides our approach to waste minimization.

We have the blueprint. Let's build together.
